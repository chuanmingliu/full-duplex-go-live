119, 142, 181, 182, 202, 282, and 623 ms: nearest-rank p50 182 ms, p95 623 ms.

Each matching SIP adapter `playback_cleared` event follows cancellation within

This measures cancellation propagation, not when already-sent sound stops at
the handset. The full call reports zero lost packets, late/duplicate packets,
and stale audio events; 129 queued frames were cleared. Rejected packets (186)
are separately reported and must not be relabeled as packet loss.
